# Embodied fly simulators and closed-loop connectome models: what to copy for the Minecraft fly

Research report, 2026-09-03. Scope: precedents for closing the loop between a connectome-derived brain model and a body in a world, and concrete recommendations for embodying the male-CNS (male-cns:v1.0, 176,422 neurons) LIF network in a Fabric 1.21.1 mod.

Confidence key: **[H]** read directly from a primary source (paper PDF/PMC full text, source code, javap); **[M]** from a search-engine summary of a primary source or from a secondary source; **[L]** inferred/estimated by me, or not verifiable.

---

## 0. Executive summary (the ten things that matter)

1. Two groups have already put a connectome-constrained fly brain in a closed sensorimotor loop: **Eon Systems (10 Mar 2026)** coupled the Shiu et al. LIF model (~140k FlyWire neurons) + the Lappalainen flyvis visual network to NeuroMechFly v2 (MuJoCo) with a **15 ms brain/body sync step**, steering through **DNa01/DNa02**, forward speed through **oDN1**, feeding through **MN9**, grooming through antennal DNs; visual activations were "decorative" (did not drive behaviour), escape was not implemented. [H] Nobody has yet used a **whole-CNS (brain + VNC)** connectome, which is the unique opportunity here: motor neurons (708 vnc_motor + 107 cb_motor) exist in the graph, so motor readout can be taken from MNs, not only DNs.
2. Every published embodiment hand-builds the same three layers: (i) a **retina renderer** (camera -> hexagonal binning), (ii) an **odor field** (inverse-square from point sources), and (iii) a **low-level body controller** (CPG gait or RL/imitation policy) driven by a **2-D descending command (left drive, right drive)**. What comes "from the brain" is the mapping sensory -> DN pattern. Copy this split. [H]
3. NeuroMechFly v2 numbers to copy: 721 ommatidia/eye on a hex grid of side 16 (radius 15); per-eye horizontal FOV ~144 deg, binocular overlap ~17 deg, total ~270 deg; fisheye correction r* = (r/zeta)/(1 - k(r^2+c^2)) with k = 3.8, zeta = 2.72; yellow:pale = 7:3 (green/blue channels); vision sampled at 500 Hz when feeding flyvis; **turning bias updated every 0.05 s** (= one Minecraft tick). [H]
4. NMF2 turning law: each side's descending scalar DN in R sets the CPG amplitude R* = |DN| and frequency nu* = +nu if DN > 0 else -nu (negative = backward stepping); nu = 12 Hz, coupling w_ij = 10, convergence alpha = 20, tripod phase biases; DN range used [0.4, 1.2] (vision) or [0.2, 1.0] (olfaction); in-place turn used [-0.2, 1.0]. Resulting speed ~13-14 mm/s. [H]
5. NMF2 olfaction law: I(d) = I_peak / d^2 at 4 sensors (2 antennae + 2 palps, weighted 9:1); s = sum_o gamma_o (I_left - I_right)/((I_left + I_right)/2), gamma_attractive = -500, gamma_aversive = +80; b = tanh(s^2) * sign(s); the inner side's drive is reduced to 1 - 0.8|b| (i.e. toward 0.2), outer side stays 1.0. [H]
6. Shiu 2024 LIF (the reference brain model, now confirmed from code): V_rest = V_reset = -52 mV, V_th = -45 mV, tau_m = 20 ms, tau_syn = 5 ms, refractory 2.2 ms, synaptic delay 1.8 ms, **w_syn = 0.275 mV per synapse** (single free parameter), GABA and glutamate inhibitory, baseline **0 Hz (silent network)**, sensory drive = Poisson 10-200 Hz; Brian2 ~5 min per 1 s trial per CPU thread; 91% of 164 predictions validated. [H]
7. DN population coding is real and linear: rotational velocity is linearly related to the **right-minus-left firing-rate difference** of steering DNs (DNa01, DNa02, DNb05, DNg13 ipsiversive; DNb06 contraversive), DN activity **leads behaviour by ~150 ms**; DNa02 = high-gain transient steering, DNa01 = low-gain sustained; bilateral DNa02 slows forward walking. Forward velocity correlates with BDN2 (= DNg100) and DNp09; MDN = backward; halting via FG/BB (GABA, SEZ) inhibiting oDN1/BDN2 and Brake (VNC) freezing legs. [H]
8. Population, not command: comDNs recruit networks (DNp09 -> 32 downstream DNs, aDN2 -> 23, MDN -> 14; median DN contacts 4 DNs; ~1,300 brain DNs). Decoding kinematics from ~100 DNs: turning R^2 ~0.6, walking speed R^2 ~0.3 (ridge regression). So decode with a **weighted linear readout over DN populations + hysteresis**, not single-neuron thresholds. [H]
9. Circuits known to work in silico from the connectome (candidates for "emergent" demos): sugar GRN -> MN9 proboscis extension (Shiu, 100% of runs at 100 Hz); JO-C/E -> aBN1/aDN1/aDN2 antennal grooming (Shiu); looming: 55 LC4 (angular velocity) + 108 LPLC2 (angular size) -> GF (DNp01) single spike -> TTMn jump; DNp03 (LC4/LPLC1/LPLC4 input) -> flight saccades and inhibition of landing DNs DNp07/DNp10; LC10a (small moving object) x P1 gain -> pursuit, ~300 net spikes/s ~= 1 rad/s turning. [H/M]
10. Time budget: Minecraft tick = 50 ms. Because the Shiu-style network is silent at baseline, an event-driven/active-set LIF at dt = 0.5-1 ms can run 50 ms of brain per tick in real time on one core for the expected activity levels (tens of thousands of synaptic events per tick); run it on a dedicated thread and hand DN/MN rates to the game thread. Keep `brainMsPerTick` configurable (50 = real time; 25 = 2x slow motion). [L, estimates in section 10]

## 1. NeuroMechFly v2 / FlyGym (Wang-Chen et al., Nature Methods 2024)

Sources: postprint PDF https://www.epfl.ch/labs/ramdya-lab/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/NMF2_postprint.pdf (read pages 1-46 incl. Methods + Supplementary Notes) [H]; docs https://neuromechfly.org/ (v2, 2026) and legacy v1 docs https://nely-epfl.github.io/flygym-gymnasium/ [H]; repo https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/flygym (Apache-2.0; v2.x released March 2026; "~2x real-time on CPU, ~60x real-time on GPU via MJWarp") [M].

### 1.1 Vision
- One colour camera per compound eye (MuJoCo rectilinear render); each image is transformed into **721 bins on a hexagonal grid** ("hexagonally arranged ommatidia grid with a side length of 16 ommatidia", i.e. radius 15: 3*15*16+1 = 721). [H]
- **FOV per eye ~144 deg horizontal, overlap ~17 deg, total ~270 deg** (Extended Data Fig. 3). [H] (Biology: ~700-750 ommatidia/eye, interommatidial angle ~4.5-4.6 deg, acceptance angle ~5 deg, nearly 180 deg per eye; Heisenberg & Wolf 1984 [M].)
- Fisheye correction of the rectilinear image (Supp. Note 5): pixel (r, c) normalised to [-1,1] takes the value of original pixel r* = (r/zeta)/(1 - k(r^2 + c^2)), c* = (c/zeta)/(1 - k(r^2 + c^2)), **k = 3.8, zeta = 2.72**; out-of-range -> empty; then mean intensity per ommatidium (Numba JIT). Implementation based on iFish (MIT). [H]
- Colour: **yellow-type (green channel) : pale-type (blue channel) = 7:3**, randomly assigned; observation array shape **(2 eyes, 721, 2 channels)**, values in [0,1], only one channel non-zero per ommatidium. Dorsal rim area not modelled. [H]
- Retina class (v1 API): `Retina(ommatidia_id_map, pale_type_mask, distortion_coefficient, zoom, nrows, ncols)`; methods `raw_image_to_hex_pxls`, `hex_pxls_to_human_readable`, `correct_fisheye`. [H]
- Object tracking rule (Methods eq. 2): threshold normalised intensity < 0.2 -> object mask; azimuth a_i in [0,1] from the anterior edge of each eye's FOV; **DN_i = min(max(k a_i + b, DN_min), DN_max), k = -3, b = 1, DN_min = 0.4, DN_max = 1.2**, applied only if relative object area A_i > 0.01 else 1.0; **updated every 0.05 s**. Black sphere moved at 10 mm/s. [H]
- Connectome-constrained vision (flyvis, Lappalainen): FlyGym and flyvis both use hex grids of side 16 -> one-to-one mapping (`retina_mapper.flyvis_to_flygym`); **visual experience sampled at 500 Hz and flyvis stepped at the same rate**, one frame at a time; two eyes simulated independently; best single model of the ensemble. Readout: the **34 T-shaped transmedullary cell types (T1-T5, Tm, TmY)** as optic-lobe outputs; object score chi = mean over cells of |(a - a_mean)/a_std| relative to baseline (empty arena) activity; ommatidia with **chi > 7** = object; then eq. 2 with [0.4, 1.2]. Head stabilisation (MLP from 42 leg joint angles + 6 contacts -> neck roll/pitch, PD gain 500) was necessary on rugged terrain. [H]

### 1.2 Olfaction
- Virtual odour sensors at the **two antennae and two maxillary palps**; odour space is k-dimensional (observation shape (k, 4)). [H]
- Diffusion: user-defined; default **I(d) = I_peak / d^2**, multiple sources summed. Attractive odour: antenna and palp averaged **9:1** (ORN ratio); aversive: antennae only (10:0). [H]
- Turning bias (Methods eq. 3-6 and tutorial code): s = sum_o gamma_o (I_left,o - I_right,o)/((I_left,o + I_right,o)/2); **gamma_attractive = -500, gamma_aversive = 80**; b = tanh(s^2) * sign(s); `control_signal = [1,1]; side = int(b > 0); control_signal[side] -= 0.8*|b|` (so DN in [0.2, 1.0]); **decision interval 0.05 s; physics dt 1e-4 s**. [H] (The printed eq. 5 "DN_inner = b(DN_max - DN_min)" is ambiguous; the tutorial code above is the operative definition.)
- RL variant: odour intensity passed through **sqrt** to linearise with distance before feeding the MLP. [H]
- Complex plume (Supp. Note 10): 2-D Navier-Stokes in PhiFlow, wind u = [1,0] with Brownian perturbation, 1 mm velocity grid, 0.5 mm odour grid, dt 1/140 s. Tracking algorithm after Demir 2020: turns as Poisson with fixed rate, **p_upwind = 1/(1+exp(-alpha_turn w_freq)), alpha_turn = 0.8 Hz^-1, w_freq = exponential filter tau_turn = 2 s of encounter train; in-place turn duration 0.25 s; lambda_walk->stop = 0.78 s^-1 baseline, -0.8 s^-1 after an encounter recovering with tau 0.2 s; lambda_stop->walk = 0.5 s^-1 + 1 s^-1 x integrated encounters (tau 0.52 s)**. Success 9/100 trials (comparable to real flies). [H]

### 1.3 Locomotion, turning, adhesion
- 42 actuated leg DoFs (7/leg), preprogrammed step (median step **0.135 s**) from 3-view video of an untethered fly; 6 coupled oscillators (Supp. Note 4): theta_dot_i = 2 pi nu_i + sum_j r_j w_ij sin(theta_j - theta_i - phi_ij); r_dot_i = alpha_i (R_i - r_i); **nu = 12 Hz, R = 1, w_ij = 10 (i != j), alpha = 20**; tripod phase biases pi (rows: RF, RM, RH, LF, LM, LH: [0 1 0 1 0 1; 1 0 1 0 1 0; ...]); tetrapod 2pi/3, wave pi/3. [H]
- **Turning (Methods eq. 1): R*_i = |DN|, nu*_i = nu if DN > 0 else -nu** (negative drive = backward stepping on that side). Biology cited: smooth turns (<20 deg) by outer-leg stroke amplitude; sharper (20-50 deg) also inner-leg amplitude decrease; in-place (>50 deg) inner legs step backward (Strauss & Heisenberg 1990). [H]
- Hybrid controller rules: overstretch (leg tip > 0.05 mm below the third-most-extended leg) and stumbling (contact force > 1 mN opposing heading during swing) -> joint adjustments (Supp. Table 5), persistence 0.002 s. [H]
- **Adhesion force 40 mN** (binary on in stance / off in swing), joint position gain kp = 45; without adhesion the fly slips at 30 deg slope, with it >90 deg. [H]
- Speeds: CPG controller ~14 mm/s on flat terrain; tutorial 27.34 mm in 2 s (13.7 mm/s); physics dt 1e-4 s, ~8,400 it/s on CPU. [H]
- Ascending path integration: heading change predicted from left-right stride-length differences (R^2 0.96), forward displacement from sums (R^2 0.98), tau = 0.64 s window. Head stabilisation: 48-D input -> 2-D neck (roll, pitch), MLP 2x32 ReLU. In-place turns for training used **DN = [-0.2, 1.0]** (tripod/tetrapod) or [0.4, 1.0] (wave); head-stab sampling DN_inner = max(1 - 0.6|b|, 0.4), DN_outer = min(1 + 0.2|b|, 1.2). [H]
- Fly following demo (Video 14): a "leading" fly walks a preset circle; the "following" fly uses flyvis + object score + eq. 2 + hybrid CPG; works on flat terrain without head stabilisation, needs it on blocks terrain. Using only T2, T2a, T3, Tm, TmY (upstream of LC9/LC10) gives similar results. [H]

## 2. Vaxenburg et al. 2025, "Whole-body physics simulation of fruit fly locomotion" (Nature 643:1312-1320; flybody)

Source: PMC12310536 full text [H]; repo https://github.com/TuragaLab/flybody (fruitfly.xml) [M].
- Body: **67 rigid bodies, 102 DoFs, 66 joints**, mass **0.983 mg** (head 0.15, thorax 0.34, abdomen 0.38, leg 0.0162, wing 0.008 mg), **length 2.97 mm, wingspan 6.04 mm**; torque actuators for wings, position actuators elsewhere, **adhesion actuators at tarsal tips and labrum with force = one body weight (~0.96 dyn)**. [H]
- Fluid model: added mass, viscous drag/resistance, Magnus lift, Kutta lift; fluidcoef = [1.0, 0.5, 1.5, 1.7, 1.0]. [H]
- Flight controller: wing-beat pattern generator + MLP; **62-D input (proprio + vestibular + steering command)**, 12-D output (wing torques, head, abdomen, WPG frequency); **baseline 218 Hz +/- 10%**; trained by imitation of 272 D. hydei trajectories (~53 s) incl. saccades; median position error 0.25 mm, orientation <5 deg. [H]
- Walking controller: pure MLP, 286-D proprio input, **59-D action (leg DoFs + adhesion + abdomen + head)**; imitation of ~16,000 snippets (~80 min) at **0-4 cm/s**; >=3 legs in stance (3.1 at 4 cm/s, 3.9 at 1 cm/s); median position error 0.4 cm, orientation 4 deg. Physics dt 0.05 ms (flight)/0.1 ms; control dt **0.2 ms flight, 2 ms walking**. [H]
- **Vision-guided flight: per-eye camera 32x32 pixels, 150 deg FOV, 4.6 deg inter-ommatidial angle**; frozen low-level flight policy + trainable CNN+MLP navigator; extra 2-D task command (**height and speed targets**). Bumps task: median height error 0.045 cm; trench task 0.032 cm. [H]
- Design lesson: a **reusable low-level controller that accepts a small steering vector** (speed, turn, height) is the interface; the high-level module never touches joints. [H]

## 3. Lappalainen et al. 2024, flyvis (Nature 634:1-10) — hex-lattice rendering

Sources: PMC11525180 [H]; code eye.py https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TuragaLab/flyvis/main/flyvis/datasets/rendering/eye.py [H].
- Network: **45,669 neurons, 64 cell types, 1,513,231 synapses, 734 free parameters**, threshold-linear dynamics tau_i dV_i/dt = -V_i + sum s_ij + V_rest + e_i, tau init 50 ms (clamped >= dt), V_rest ~ N(0.5, 0.05). **721 columns** = hexagonal extent 15. [H]
- Rendering: `BoxEye(extent=15, kernel_size=13)` = mean of a 13x13 pixel box per receptor, receptor centres 13 px apart on the hex lattice; `HexEye(n_ommatidia=721, ppo=25)` with **omm_width = omm_height = 5.8 deg**. Input is **normalised greyscale in [0,1]**, mean luminance 0.5 background; "the transduced luminance at each photoreceptor is the greyscale mean value in the 13x13-pixel region surrounding it"; **no explicit contrast normalisation** beyond this. [H]
- Time step: 5 ms integration reported in the PMC text (frames from Sintel 24 fps, 19-frame sequences); direction selectivity measured at speeds 13.92-145 deg/s. [M — verify flyvis default dt in code; may be 1/50 s.]
- The 721-column visual field therefore spans ~ (2*15+1) * 5.8 deg ~ 180 deg (rough). [L]

## 4. Shiu et al. 2024, whole-brain LIF (Nature 634:210-219)

Sources: PMC11446845 [H]; code https://github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model model.py (cached copy inspected) [H]; Eon multi-backend port https://github.com/eonsystemspbc/fly-brain (Brian2, Brian2CUDA, PyTorch, NEST GPU, GeNN; FlyWire v783, ~138k neurons, 0.1 ms dt) [M].
- Equations (Brian2): dv/dt = (v_0 - v + g)/t_mbr; dg/dt = -g/tau; on_pre: g += w; reset: v = v_rst, g = 0. **v_0 = v_rst = -52 mV, v_th = -45 mV, t_mbr = 20 ms, tau = 5 ms, t_rfc = 2.2 ms, t_dly = 1.8 ms, w_syn = 0.275 mV** x (synapse count) x (+1 excitatory / -1 inhibitory); default Poisson rate 150 Hz on driven neurons (refractory set to 0 for driven neurons). [H]
- Neurotransmitter sign: neuron inhibitory if >50% of presynaptic sites predicted GABA or glutamate; dopamine/octopamine/serotonin treated as excitatory; making glutamate excitatory raised the false-positive rate from 1% to 16%. [H]
- Network is **silent at baseline (0 Hz)**; 127,400 FlyWire v630 neurons; 1,000 ms trials x 30; "activated" = any spike; ~5 min per trial per CPU thread. [H]
- Results to reproduce as demos: sugar GRNs (10-200 Hz) -> MN6/8/9/11; MN9 fires in 100% of runs at 100 Hz (1/100 with shuffled weights); contralateral MN9 stronger than ipsilateral for unilateral input; water GRNs -> MN9; bitter and Ir94e GRNs inhibit MN9; JO-CE -> aBN1/aBN2/aDN1/aDN2 (antennal grooming), JO-F does not. 91% of 164 predictions correct. [H]
- Threshold arithmetic for design: 7 mV above rest / 0.275 mV = **~26 coincident unitary synapses** needed to spike a neuron from rest (before decay). [L]

## 5. Lobato-Rios et al. 2022, NeuroMechFly v1 (Nature Methods 19:620-627)

Source: PubMed/GitHub README (https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/NeuroMechFly) [M]; details in NMF2 text [H].
- PyBullet physics; morphology from X-ray microtomography; kinematic replay of tethered walking/grooming to infer contact forces; CPG network (FARMS, salamander-derived oscillators) + Ekeberg-style muscles; NSGA-style multi-objective gait optimisation ('fastest', 'tradeoff', 'most_stable'). [M]
- Limitation that motivated v2: tethered kinematics looked unnatural untethered; no vision/olfaction; no adhesion. [H]

## 6. Connectome-in-the-loop robots and other embodiments

### 6.1 OpenWorm / Busbice C. elegans LEGO & GoPiGo robots
Sources: https://github.com/Connectome/GoPiGo connectome.py [H, partial]; Valencia Urbina, Cannas & Gleiser 2020, arXiv:2011.09057 (PDF read) [H]; Elektor/Hackaday articles [M].
- 302 neurons, ~7,000 synapses, integrate-and-fire with **threshold = 30** in GoPiGo code (Elektor article: "threshold 10, counter reset after 200 ms"); each spike adds synapse-count weights to postsynaptic accumulators. [H/M]
- **Motor readout: body-wall muscles MDL07-23, MVL07-23 (mLeft) and MDR07-23, MVR07-23 (mRight) accumulate weights each cycle; left and right wheel speeds are set from the accumulated left/right muscle sums** (sign gives direction). Sonar -> "nose touch" sensory neurons; food sensor -> chemosensory neurons. [H/M]
- Emergent: nose touch stops/reverses, anterior/posterior touch -> forward/back; in the Gleiser robot (update **1 Hz**, max signal frequency 0.5 Hz): frequency-synchronised neuron clusters (0.165/0.332/0.496 Hz), nested dynamics, attractor-like PC trajectories whose segments map to forward/backward/turn/stop; neurons with largest positive PC1 (DA7, VA11, VA8) promote forward, most negative (VD7, VD6, AVAL) promote backward — mirroring Kato et al. 2015 in the real worm. [H]
- Lesson: even a crude weighted sum of motor-pool activity -> wheel speeds yields behaviour that correlates with real-animal neural dynamics; the readout itself is trivial, the connectome provides the structure. [H]

### 6.2 Zebrafish
- No connectome-in-the-loop zebrafish exists (no complete larval connectome yet; State of Brain Emulation Report 2025, arXiv:2510.15745 [M]). "Cook et al. zebrafish" could not be located [not verified]. Closest: embodied RL zebrafish agents (arXiv:2506.00138): 6-link MuJoCo swimmer, 5 torque DoFs, **64x64 egocentric camera**, LSTM policy, intrinsic motivation; predicts whole-brain neural/glial activity. [M]

### 6.3 Eon Systems embodied fly (10 Mar 2026)
Source: https://eon.systems/updates/embodied-brain-emulation [H].
- Brain: Shiu LIF (~140k neurons, ~50M synapses) + Lappalainen visual network (activations "piped in" to corresponding LIF neurons; currently decorative). Body: NeuroMechFly v2 (87 joints, MuJoCo). **Sync every 15 ms** ("may be too slow for some behaviors").
- Interface: "sparse descending-neuron interface as control handles": **DNa01/DNa02 -> steering, oDN1 -> forward velocity, antennal DNs -> grooming, MN9 -> feeding**; imitation-learned NMF controllers execute joint trajectories. Taste sources = food; virtual dust -> antennal mechanosensory neurons.
- Behaviours shown: wander -> detect food (taste cue) -> approach -> proboscis extension/eat; groom when dusty. Escape not implemented in the body. They explicitly say sensorimotor mappings were chosen manually and this is not yet a proof that structure alone suffices.

### 6.4 Other
- erojasoficial-byte/fly-brain (GitHub, unreviewed hobby project): FlyWire v783 138,639 neurons/15.1M synapses in PyTorch (tau_m 10 ms, tau_syn 5 ms, thr -50, reset -65, dt 0.2 ms, Hebbian plasticity), `brain_body_bridge.py` decodes ~1,100 DN rates into walk/turn/escape/groom/feed/flight for NMF2; claims not validated — treat as an architecture sketch only. [M/L]
- Jin, Zhu, Zhang, Sui 2026, arXiv:2602.17997 "Whole-Brain Connectomic Graph Model Enables Whole-Body Locomotion Control": connectome wiring used as a GNN controller trained by RL (not spiking); better sample efficiency than baselines. [M]
- Neuromorphic port of the Drosophila connectome on Loihi 2 (arXiv:2508.16792). [M]

## 7. Descending-neuron population coding: evidence for continuous speed/turn decoding

- **Aymanns, Chen & Ramdya 2022 (eLife 11:e81527)**: 75-95 DNs imaged per fly in the cervical connective (~16 Hz); **~60% of DNs best encode walking, ~15% head grooming**, few resting, none best encode front-leg rubbing/posterior movements; ridge regression: **turning R^2 ~0.6, walking speed R^2 ~0.3** within walking epochs; turn-encoding DNs ipsilaterally enriched; DNs encode high-level behaviours, joint angles add little; odour (ACV, MSC) encoded by few DNs independent of behaviour. [H]
- **Braun, Hurtak, Wang-Chen & Ramdya 2024 (Nature 630:686-694)**: comDNs recruit DN networks: **DNp09 -> 32 downstream DNs (forward walking), aDN2 -> 23 (antennal grooming), MDN -> 14 (backward)**; across ~1,300 brain DNs the median DN connects to 4 others, some >80; broadcaster DNs (DNp42, aDN1, DNa01, DNa02, DNb02) vs standalone DNs (oviDN, DNg11, Mute, DNg14) that drive simple movements (abdominal dipping, ovipositor extension) even after decapitation; within-cluster excitation, between-cluster inhibition. [H]
- **Yang et al. 2024 (Cell, "Fine-grained descending control of steering")**: **DNa01, DNa02, DNb05, DNg13 ipsiversive; DNb06 contraversive; right-left activity difference linearly correlated with rotational velocity; activity leads by ~150 ms**; DNa02 stride-locked (~15 Hz modulation), DNg13 slower; unilateral DNa02 200 ms pulse -> ipsiversive turn without forward-velocity change; bilateral DNa02 -> slower forward walking. [H]
- **Rayshubskiy et al. 2024 (eLife 13:RP102230)**: DNa02 = high-gain transient steering (direct PFL3 input from the central complex; contralateral inhibition via DNa03/LAL010 = "see-saw"), DNa01 = low-gain sustained steering; **rotational velocity essentially linear in right-minus-left firing across the dynamic range**; DNa02 encodes laterality of the action, not the stimulus (ipsilateral to attractive odour, contralateral to aversive heat). [H]
- **Sapkal et al. 2024 (Nature 634, halting)**: Foxglove and Bluebell (GABAergic SEZ neurons) implement walk-OFF by inhibiting walking DNs (**FG -> oDN1 and BDN2; BB -> most P9-recruited DNs**); Brake = 6 cholinergic ascending VNC neurons that lock joints in stance; **BDN2 (= DNg100) activity correlates with forward but not angular velocity**; the paper also used a connectome-constrained whole-brain spiking model. [H]
- **Liessem ... Ache 2026 (Curr Biol, "Control of walking direction by descending and dopaminergic neurons")**: MDN integrates antennal touch to switch forward->backward; dopaminergic **DopaMeander** neurons drive forward walking with increased (ipsiversive) turning; both **gated out during flight**. [M]
- **"A dedicated brain circuit controls forward walking" (bioRxiv Jan 2026)**: central-brain neurons recruit a specific DN population controlling forward speed independently of turning; oDN1 possibly redundant/context-specific. [M]
- Landing/flight: **DNp07 and DNp10 drive the landing response (front-leg extension, middle/hind legs extend and lower) and respond to visual stimuli only during flight** [M]; **DNp03** is a flight-saccade hub (inputs LPLC1, LPLC4, LC4, LC22, LC23; strongly looming-tuned and ipsilateral-biased; outputs to DLMn/DVMn power MNs, contralateral b3/ps1/i1 MNs, ipsilateral b1 via w-cHINs, DNa04/05/15 and inhibitory DNb01; **inhibits DNp07/DNp10**) [H, PMC12977095].
- VNC side (MANC; Cheong et al. 2024 eLife 96084): **1,328 DNs; 733 MNs (392 leg MNs: 142 T1, 119 T2, 131 T3; 26 wing MNs; 7 haltere MN pairs)**; MNs receive on average only **7% of input directly from DNs**, except neck, some abdominal, several wing and haltere MNs (>20%, up to ~60%); leg neuropils 404 DNs : 392 MNs, upper tectulum 381 DNs : 106 MNs; 37 premotor communities (18 with >=50 cells). Lesser et al. 2024 (Nature, FANC): leg premotor modules with synaptic weight proportional to MN size (recruitment hierarchy); wing premotor lacks this. [H/M]

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Antennal grooming | JO-C/E -> aBN1/2 -> aDN1/aDN2 (Shiu 2024; Hampel 2015) | Dust/touch on antenna (rain, particles, block contact) -> JO-CE Poisson | aDN1/2 rate -> front-leg grooming animation | High |
| Looming escape (takeoff) | LC4 (angular velocity) + LPLC2 (angular size) -> GF/DNp01 (Ache 2019; von Reyn 2017); GF -> TTMn (~1 ms) | Retina with temporal derivative; an approaching player hand/mob expands on the retina | GF spike -> TTMn jump + wing MN raise -> flight | High if retina motion is faithful |
| Flight saccades / evasive turns | DNp03 hub, LPLC1/LC4 input | Expanding object during flight | Wing steering MN asymmetry -> yaw/roll | Medium |
| Landing | DNp07/DNp10 (visual, gated by flight state) | Expansion of surface below | Leg extension, stop flight | Medium (state gating must be supplied) |
| Odour approach (chemotaxis) | ORN -> PN -> LH -> ... -> DNa02 laterality (Rayshubskiy: DNa02 ipsi to attractive odour) | Bilateral ORN rates from inverse-square field; encounters | R-L DN difference -> yaw | Medium (LH-to-DN path is long; may need gain) |
| Upwind surge / cast | Needs wind sensing (JO-C/E deflection; WPNs) + odour ON/OFF (Alvarez-Salvado 2018; Demir 2020) | Minecraft has no wind: synthesize a breeze or use self-motion airflow | Speed up on odour ON, turn more on OFF | Low-Medium |
| Optomotor stabilisation | HS/VS -> DNp15 (DNHS1), DNp20/22 (DNOVS1/2), neck MNs via gap junctions (Fujiwara 2017; Nat Commun 2024) | Wide-field yaw optic flow from the retina | DNp15 R-L -> corrective yaw | Medium (T4/T5 motion detection must work in the LIF) |

| Phototaxis | R7/R8 -> Dm8 -> Tm5c (Dm8 essential for UV/green phototaxis); downstream DN path unknown | Sky light / torch luminance per column | forward + turn | Low |

## 9. Real-fly kinematic anchors (for scaling and calibration)

- Walking: forward speeds 0-30 mm/s, bimodal with peaks at 0 and **~17.5 mm/s** (DeAngelis 2019); typical straight walking ~10-20 mm/s; body length ~2.5-3 mm -> **4-8 body lengths/s**; walking saccades ~15 deg in 100 ms (~150 deg/s); angular speed > 10 deg/s threshold for "turning". [M]
- Flight: forward 0.11-0.20 m/s (40-80 BL/s); **saccades ~90 deg in 50 ms, peak yaw 1,800 deg/s** (Fry 2003); wingbeat ~200-220 Hz (218 Hz used by Vaxenburg). [M]
- Escape: GF -> TTM latency ~0.9-1.2 ms; short-mode takeoff < 7 ms after GF spike; one GF spike's timing selects short vs long takeoff. [M]
- Feeding: labellar spread can start ~10 ms after contact (Zhou 2019 Sci Adv); PER to sugar within hundreds of ms. [M]
- Olfaction: ORN firing up to ~300 Hz, roughly power-law/log-linear over 10^-8 to 10^-2 dilution (Hallem & Carlson 2006); Alvarez-Salvado 2018 ON filter tau 0.72 s (adaptation 9.8 s), OFF filter 0.62/4.84 s; baseline speed 6 mm/s, upwind drive 5 deg/sample; wind-blind flies still speed up on odour ON and search on OFF. [M/H]
- Demir 2020 plume walking: ~10 mm/s, stereotyped 30 +/- 10 deg saccades, ~1.3 turns/s; encounter = >2.5 SD above noise for >= 50 ms. [M]

## 10. Recommendations for the Minecraft embodiment

### (a) Rasterising a Minecraft retina cheaply
Verified mojmap API (javap on the cached 1.21.1 jar) [H]: `BlockGetter.clip(ClipContext) -> BlockHitResult`; `new ClipContext(Vec3 from, Vec3 to, ClipContext.Block.VISUAL|COLLIDER|OUTLINE, ClipContext.Fluid.NONE|ANY|WATER, Entity)`; `BlockHitResult.getBlockPos()/getDirection()/getLocation()`, `HitResult.getType()` in {MISS, BLOCK, ENTITY}; `BlockAndTintGetter.getBrightness(LightLayer.SKY|LightLayer.BLOCK, BlockPos)`, `getRawBrightness(BlockPos, int skyDarken)`, `Level.getSkyDarken()`, `canSeeSky(BlockPos)`; `BlockState.getMapColor(BlockGetter, BlockPos).col` (packed RGB int); `BlockState.getLightEmission()`; `ProjectileUtil.getEntityHitResult(Level, Entity, Vec3, Vec3, AABB, Predicate<Entity>)`; `Entity.getEyePosition()`, `getViewVector(float)`, `Vec3.directionFromRotation(float xRot, float yRot)`, `Vec3.yRot/xRot/scale/normalize/dot`.
1. Geometry: two hex retinas of **radius 8 (217 columns/eye) by default, radius 15 (721) as a config option**. Per column store an axial (q, r) coordinate and a unit ray direction in head coordinates: azimuth offset +/-72 deg (eye centres), per-column spacing = FOV/(2*radius): 144 deg / 16 = 9 deg at radius 8, 4.8 deg at radius 15 (biological 4.6 deg); elevation range +/- 60 deg; 17 deg binocular overlap (so columns near the midline exist in both eyes). Map columns onto the dataset's `assignedOlHex1/assignedOlHex2` lattice (1..36 x 1..39, ~880 columns) by nearest neighbour after rotating into the medulla frame; drive **L1/L2/L3 (and Mi1/Tm1... only via the network)** since R1-R6 lack hex coordinates; assign each R-cell the hex of its main L1/L2 target if photoreceptor-level drive is wanted.
2. Ray budget: `Level.clip` over 24 blocks costs roughly 2-20 us (DDA over <=24 cells plus shape tests) [L]. 2 x 217 rays = 434 rays/tick ~ 1-9 ms; 2 x 721 = 1,442 rays ~ 3-30 ms — too much on the server thread with several flies. Use **temporal interleaving only for static-block luminance** (e.g. 1/2 of the columns per tick, full refresh every 100 ms) and compute **entity images analytically** every tick: for each entity within 32 blocks, project its AABB corners into eye coordinates and rasterise the covered hex columns (dark object of luminance ~0.1). Entities are what LC4/LPLC2/LC10a/T4-T5 need; blocks change slowly. Ray length 24-32 blocks; MISS -> sky luminance.
3. Luminance per column: L = albedo * max(skyLight * (1 - skyDarken/15), blockLight)/15 where skyLight/blockLight come from `getBrightness(LightLayer.SKY/BLOCK, hitPos.relative(face))` and albedo from MapColor.col -> per-channel (yellow ommatidia use G, pale use B, 7:3 mask) normalised to [0,1]; add `getRainLevel` dimming. This reproduces flyvis' greyscale-in-[0,1] convention.
4. Temporal processing: keep the previous frame per column; feed photoreceptor/lamina input as **rate = r_max * L (r_max ~ 100-150 Hz Poisson) plus a high-passed term** (L_t - L_{t-1}) split into ON (L1 pathway) and OFF (L2/L3) components, because the LIF network has no phototransduction adaptation; without a derivative term the silent network saturates under bright sky. Column update rate = tick rate (20 Hz); flyvis used 500 Hz and the fly's flicker fusion is ~100 Hz, so 20 Hz is coarse but adequate for looming (l/v 10-80 ms stimuli still span 2-10 ticks) [L]. If finer motion is needed, interpolate entity positions sub-tick on the brain thread (entities have continuous positions).
5. Budget target: <= 2 ms/tick per fly for vision on the server thread (analytic entity raster + 217 half-refreshed block rays), <= 0.5 ms for olfaction/taste, brain on its own thread.

### (b) Olfaction and taste from items/blocks
1. Odour sources: `ItemEntity.getItem()`; food if `stack.get(DataComponents.FOOD)` returns `FoodProperties` (nutrition(), saturation()) [H]; map a small table item -> ORN class vector (e.g. apple/sweet berries/melon -> fruity esters -> ORN_DM1/DM4/VA2; rotten flesh -> ORN_DL4/"bad" acids -> aversive; honey/fermented -> ORN_VA2/DC1 like ACV; cake -> sugar volatiles). Blocks: cake, melon, sweet-berry bush, composter, campfire smoke (aversive), water (HRN humidity), lava (TRN heat). Peak intensity I_peak = nutrition (or fixed per class); **I(d) = I_peak / max(d, d_min)^2** summed over sources (NMF2 default) with d in blocks and d_min = 0.5.
2. Sampling: two virtual antennae offset +/-0.5 block laterally from the head (exaggerated versus the real ~0.3 mm separation so the R-L difference is informative) and two palps (9:1 weighting); pass through **sqrt or log compression** (NMF2 RL variant used sqrt) and then a Hill function per ORN class (n ~ 1, K spaced logarithmically) to a Poisson rate capped at ~250 Hz; add ORN adaptation (high-pass with tau ~0.7 s ON / 0.6 s OFF per Alvarez-Salvado) so encounters, not steady levels, drive the network.
3. Wind: Minecraft has none. Options in order of preference: (i) a **global breeze vector per world** (slow OU process, stronger during rain), used both to advect odour (shift source position downwind by k * distance for a plume-like cone and add temporal intermittency via Perlin noise gated at a threshold to make discrete encounters as in Demir 2020) and to stimulate JO-C/E wind_gravity neurons by antenna deflection = breeze projected on the head frame; (ii) self-motion airflow (fly velocity reversed) during flight, which real flies use for airspeed; (iii) no wind at all — chemotaxis then relies on bilateral gradients only (works in NMF2 with gamma = -500).
4. Taste: contact test each tick between the proboscis/tarsi hitbox and food items/blocks -> labellar (LB*) and tarsal (LgLG*) sugar GRNs at 50-150 Hz (Shiu's validated range) while in contact; bitter GRNs for rotten flesh/poisonous potato/spider eye; water GRNs on water contact/rain. Hygrosensory/thermosensory from biome temperature (`Biome.getBaseTemperature()`) and `Level.isRainingAt`.
5. Mechanosensation: rain drops / block collisions -> BM_* head bristles and JO-CE (grooming trigger); leg SNta bristles on ground contact; SNpp/SApp proprioceptive from the body controller's own gait phase (closes the ascending loop like NMF2's path-integration signals).

### (c) Motor decoding architecture (brain -> body)
Layer 1, spike-rate estimation: exponential moving average of spike counts per neuron with **tau = 100-150 ms** (matches the 150 ms DN->behaviour lead), updated every brain step; expose per-type sums (L and R separately).
Layer 2, high-level command vector u = (v_fwd, yaw_rate, v_z/flight, discrete actions):
- **yaw_rate = g_turn * sum_k w_k (r_R,k - r_L,k)** over steering populations with weights w: DNa02 = 1.0 (high gain), DNa01 = 0.4, DNb05 = 0.5, DNg13 = 0.5, DNb06 = -0.5 (contraversive), DNp09 = 0.3 (ipsiversive component), plus in flight DNp03 = 1.0 and DNa04/05/15 = 0.3. Calibrate g_turn so a 50 Hz R-L difference in DNa02 gives ~200 deg/s walking (Hindmarsh Sten anchor: 300 net spikes/s ~ 57 deg/s for LC10a; Yang: linear).
- **v_fwd = g_fwd * (DNp09 + BDN2(DNg100) + oDN1(if present) + BDN1/3/4 + DopaMeander-like) - g_back * MDN(4 cells) - g_halt * (Brake ascending + FG/BB inhibition already acts in-network)**; clamp to [-0.5, 1] x v_max; a negative value = backward walking (MDN). Real forward speed correlates with BDN2 (Sapkal) and DNp09 (Bidaye/Braun).
- Flight state: enter on a **GF (DNp01) spike or DNp02/DNp04/DNp11 takeoff DN burst** or sustained wing power MN (DLMn/DVMn) activity > theta for 50 ms; exit on **DNp07/DNp10 landing rate > theta for >= 100 ms while a surface is within 1 block below**, or when wing MN activity decays for 300 ms. In flight, yaw from DNp03/DNa04/05/15 and wing steering MNs (b1/b2 up-strokes vs b3/i1/ps1 down), pitch/altitude from hg/tp MNs (low confidence; use a hand-built hover-altitude controller with DNp07/10 as "descend").
- Discrete actions with hysteresis and refractory periods: **PER** when MN9 (plus MN6/8/11) rate > 20 Hz for 100 ms (Shiu criterion: MN9 firing) -> 1 s proboscis animation, consume nutrition; **grooming** when aDN1/aDN2 (DNg62/DNge078) > theta for 200 ms -> front-leg grooming loop until rate < theta/2 for 500 ms; **jump** on the first GF spike (single-spike event; 5 s refractory); **song** when pIP10 (or pC1/P1 + vPR6 wing MN pattern) exceeds theta -> play a 35 ms IPI pulse-train sound (Level.playSound with a custom SoundEvent) with unilateral wing extension; **halt** when Brake/FG/BB dominate (v_fwd -> 0 within 100 ms).
- Read MNs where they exist (this dataset has the VNC): leg MN pools per neuromere give a second, more "emergent" estimate of gait drive (sum of T1-T3 depressor/extensor/flexor MN rates -> v_fwd; left-right asymmetry -> yaw). Blend: u = 0.7*DN estimate + 0.3*MN estimate initially; move weight to MNs if VNC premotor circuits turn out to oscillate under DN drive (they may not: MANC premotor rhythm generation is not guaranteed in a silent LIF).
Layer 3, body "spinal" controller (hand-built, NMF2-style): 6 phase oscillators (**nu = 12 Hz baseline scaled by |v_fwd|, R = |drive|, sign of drive reverses phase direction**, tripod phase biases, coupling 10, convergence 20) -> per-leg swing/stance phase -> leg animation (in a Fabric client EntityRenderer/Model) and stance-based displacement (Entity.setDeltaMovement, setYRot; MoveControl/FlyingMoveControl for pathing if the target is a wanted position). Wings: flapping at a fixed visual rate (~10 Hz visual proxy of 218 Hz) while in flight. Adhesion analogue: allow walking on walls/ceilings when contact normal exists (optional).
Layer 4, safety: clamp yaw rate (300 deg/s walking, 720 deg/s flight), forward speed (see (d)), rate-limit state changes (min dwell 250 ms), and add a low-level "reflex" layer for collisions (NMF2's stumbling/overstretch rules) so the mob never gets stuck regardless of brain output.

### (d) Fly size and speed scale
- Real fly: 2.5-3 mm, 0.98 mg. A 0.1-block fly is invisible in a README video and unrenderable at distance. Recommend **body length 0.5 block (hitbox 0.5 x 0.3), scale factor ~170x**, config `flyScale` allowing 0.25 (cat-sized). Keep speeds in **body lengths per second**: walking 4-8 BL/s -> **2-4 blocks/s** (player walk 4.3 blocks/s), in-place turn up to 300 deg/s, walking saccades 15-30 deg in 100 ms; flight real 40-80 BL/s would be 20-40 blocks/s — **cap at 8-12 blocks/s** (game-tolerable, comparable to bees/parrots) and saccades 90 deg in 50 ms (one tick). Odour detection radius ~16-24 blocks (scaled), vision ray length 24-32 blocks; item pickup/taste contact at 0.4 blocks.
- Because scale x160 makes the world's spatial frequencies coarse relative to the eye, interommatidial spacing of 9 deg (radius-8 retina) is a sensible match; the 721-column option is for fidelity demos.

### (e) Brain time per game tick
- Anchors: NMF2 turning-decision interval 0.05 s; Eon sync 15 ms; flyvis 5 ms; Shiu dt (Brian2 default 0.1 ms), synaptic delay 1.8 ms, tau_m 20 ms; DN->behaviour lead 150 ms; GF->muscle ~1 ms.
- Recommendation: **default 50 ms of brain per 50 ms tick (real time)** with **dt = 0.5 ms** (delay 1.8 ms ~ 4 steps; refractory 2.2 ms ~ 4 steps; exact exponential decay of v and g so dt does not change fixed points). Implement as an **active-set LIF**: only neurons with g != 0 or v != v_rest are updated (the network is silent at baseline), spikes pushed to a delay ring buffer, synapses stored CSR by presynaptic neuron (6.29M edges at weight >= 5; 25.86M total — start with >= 5 or >= 3 and scale w_syn as Shiu did, 0.275 mV x count). Cost estimate: with ~10-20k active neurons and ~5-10k spikes per 50 ms, synaptic events ~0.2-1.5M per tick (mean out-degree 36 at w>=5, ~147 for all edges) -> ~2-15 ms of Java on one core [L]. Run on a dedicated thread with double-buffered rate vectors; the game thread reads the latest rates at tick start, writes sensory rates at tick end (one-tick latency = 50 ms, within the biological 150 ms DN lead).
- Fallbacks: if activity explodes (98k cholinergic neurons), add a global divisive normalisation or lower w_syn to 0.2 mV, not slow motion; if the GF pathway needs ms-precision, use dt = 0.1 ms only for a tagged sub-population. Provide `brainMsPerTick` (50 real time; 25 = half speed "bullet time" for demos) and `brainDt`.

## 11. Emergent vs hand-built (design contract)

| Comes from the connectome (emergent) | Must be hand-built in the body/world |
|---|---|
| Which DNs/MNs light up for a given sensory pattern (taste->MN9; JO->aDN; looming->GF/DNp03; odour laterality->DNa02; LC10a->P1->pIP10) | Retina rasteriser, hex mapping, luminance normalisation, temporal derivative |
| Left-right steering asymmetry, forward vs backward antagonism (DNp09/BDN2 vs MDN), halting (FG/BB/Brake) | Odour field, breeze, encounter statistics, ORN dose-response and adaptation |
| Behavioural sequencing among DN clusters (within-cluster excitation, between-cluster inhibition) | Gait CPG / leg & wing animation, adhesion, collision reflexes |
| Population coding gains (relative) | Absolute gains DN rate -> blocks/s and deg/s; hysteresis; min dwell; refractory |
| Sensory-specific pathways (bitter suppresses feeding) | Internal state (hunger, P1 arousal, flight-state gating of MDN/landing DNs), spontaneous activity/noise, neuromodulation |
| Single-spike GF decision | Takeoff/landing physics, flight altitude control |

## 12. Open questions / risks
1. Does a silent, Poisson-driven LIF of the whole CNS produce sustained DN activity at behaviourally meaningful rates (10-100 Hz) from realistic sensory rates, or will DN output be sparse bursts? Shiu drove only ~10s of GRNs; ~700 ORNs plus ~3,400 R1-R6 at 100 Hz is a much larger drive — risk of runaway excitation. Needs empirical tuning of w_syn/synapse threshold and possibly a shared inhibitory bias.
2. Will T4/T5 direction selectivity emerge in a LIF with count-based weights and no delays beyond 1.8 ms? flyvis needed learned tau/rest parameters. If not, looming (LC4/LPLC2) may still work from lamina ON/OFF transients, but optomotor will not.
3. VNC premotor rhythm generation: does DN drive to the MANC-like VNC produce alternating leg MN bursts? Probably not without intrinsic bursting; hence the hand-built CPG. Test with DNp09 100 Hz drive and inspect leg MN pool rates.
4. Exact flyvis time step (5 vs 20 ms) and the Ache 2019 Gaussian parameters (size tuning centre/width) were not obtained.
5. Does the male-CNS dataset include oDN1 and DopaMeander under those names? (BDN2 = DNg100 confirmed by orchestrator; check synonyms.)
6. Cost of `Level.clip` per ray in 1.21.1 was estimated, not measured; measure before fixing the retina radius.
7. Client-server split: rates must be synced to clients for the dashboard/animation (custom payload packets; ~50 floats/tick is fine).
